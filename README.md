# 🪡 DarziDost — The Digital Tailoring Revolution

**DarziDost** is a premium, offline-first mobile application designed specifically for master tailors (artisans) to manage their entire workshop with digital precision. No more bulky paper registers — manage customers, measurements, and order progress from the palm of your hand.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Customer Digital Register
Store your entire client base in one place. Search instantly by name, code, or phone number.
- **Unique Customer Codes**: Assign and track customers by their shop-specific IDs.
- **Call Integration**: One-tap call to any customer directly from their profile.

### 📏 Measurement Vault
Never lose a measurement again. Store detailed dimensions for every suit type.
- **Template System**: Pre-defined templates for common suits (Shalwar Kameez, Waistcoats, etc.).
- **Revision History**: Keep track of measurement changes over time.
- **Custom Notes**: Add specific tailoring instructions per customer.

### 👔 Order & Suit Tracking
Manage your production workflow from cutting to delivery.
- **Status Badges**: Real-time tracking (Pending, Ready, Delivered).
- **Advance/Total Tracking**: Keep a clear log of payments for every suit.
- **Due Dates**: Never miss a deadline with organized order sorting.

### 🌍 Global Support (v1.7.1)
- **International Phone Input**: Support for tailors and customers worldwide with country-specific validation and flag selection.
- **Multilingual UI**: Native support for **English** and **Urdu (اردو)**.

### 🔒 Secure & Private
- **PIN Authentication**: Your data stays private with a secure 4-digit PIN lock.
- **Offline First**: Works perfectly without an internet connection using local database syncing.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Framework**: React Native (Android/iOS)
- **Database**: WatermelonDB (Offline-first, reactive)
- **Cloud Sync**: Supabase (Remote backup and synchronization)
- **Phone Validation**: libphonenumber-js
- **Styling**: Vanilla StyleSheet with dynamic themes
